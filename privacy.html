Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Welcome to racun cuan.id ("we", "our", or "the Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how our personal content management tool handles data and respects your privacy when connecting with third-party platforms such as Pinterest.

1. Information We Collect

racun cuan.id operates primarily as a local client-side tool and automation suite. We collect and store:

  • Pinterest API Tokens: Developer Bearer tokens provided directly by the account owner to authenticate with Pinterest API v5.
  • Public Product & Pin Data: Titles, descriptions, images, and public product links curated by the user for Pinterest publishing.
  • Local Campaign Configurations: User-defined prompt matrix settings and board mappings stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Pinterest Data

We access and use Pinterest APIs strictly to provide authorized functionality requested by the user, including:

  • Creating and scheduling Pins to the user's authorized Pinterest boards via POST /v5/pins.
  • Retrieving the user's board listings via GET /v5/boards to allow board selection.
  • Retrieving pin performance analytics (impressions, clicks, saves) to display insights in the user's personal dashboard.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your Pinterest account data or personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

All sensitive credentials (such as API tokens and webhook configurations) are stored securely on the user's local instance. No external third-party tracking or behavioral advertising cookies are utilized.

4. Third-Party Services

Our tool interacts with official APIs provided by Pinterest and Google (Gemini AI). Your interaction with these third-party platforms is subject to their respective terms and privacy policies:

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via your connected Pinterest account or developer contact channel.
